# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who write across desktop applications and want fast, private voice input and proofreading without sending audio or text to a cloud service. They often invoke Echo while focused in another application, so controls must remain understandable without interrupting their work.

## Product Purpose

Echo turns speech into text and conservatively polishes selected text using local models. Success means a user can invoke either action instantly by shortcut or a subtle resident control, understand model readiness, and return to writing without managing an AI workflow.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, immediate, trustworthy. Echo should feel native to the desktop, technically confident, and respectful of attention.

## Anti-references

Bulky always-open assistants, floating toolbars that obscure content, decorative glass effects that compete with the task, and multi-mode AI interfaces that make a simple action feel configurable or ambiguous.

## Design Principles

- Stay present without demanding attention.
- Reveal capability progressively at the point of use.
- Make local privacy and readiness legible through behavior, not marketing.
- Keep shortcuts and visible controls equivalent.
- Prefer immediate, reversible feedback over configuration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard access. Every icon-only action needs an accessible name and visible focus state. Motion must communicate state, remain brief, and respect reduced-motion preferences. Status must never depend on color alone.
